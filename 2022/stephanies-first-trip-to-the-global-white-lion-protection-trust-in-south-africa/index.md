## Animal Safari

This was our very first Lion Monitoring Safari.  The Trust does two Lion monitoring Safari’s every day.  We were very blessed to have seen Matsieng and Zukhara on our very first drive.  Every day at the trust was a new adventure.  Seeing so many animals being protected by the Trust.
