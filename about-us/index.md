## Our Vision

Respect and freedom for all of Earth’s inhabitants.

To create opportunities for learning about and appreciating the importance of our relationship as humans with animals and the profound impact of that relationship on the sustainability of life on Earth.

## Mission Statement

To educate all those individuals passionate and dedicated to conserving wildlife and their environment in an inclusive and accessible way.

## History

Stephanie's Big Cat Quest is about respect, courage and love. We need courage to make the changes needed to prevent big cat extinction. We need respect for the animals, the world and the people that are fighting for their rights and we need love to give us the determination and motivation to succeed.

We are a dedicated and driven team, based in Perth, Western Australia with a mission to save the world’s big cats from extinction. We began in 2013, after our co-founders Stephanie and her parents Angy and John visited South Africa to volunteer at the Global White Lion Protection Trust in Timbavati, Greater Kruger.

Since then Stephanie has returned 6 times to visit GWLPT, taking other volunteers from Australia to help out.

## Our Values

Integrity, Respect, Creativity, Collaboration, and Authenticity.
