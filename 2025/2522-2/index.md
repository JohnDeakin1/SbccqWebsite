## **Privacy Policy**

### **1\. Introduction**

Stephanie’s Big Cat Conservation Quest (SBCCQ) is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals and ensuring the security of personal information. This policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and disclose personal information in compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

### **2\. Collection of Personal Information**

We collect personal information that is necessary for our functions and activities. This may include:

- **Contact Information:** Name, email address, postal address, and phone number.
- **Newsletter Subscriptions:** Email addresses collected via our newsletter sign-up forms.
- **Marketplace Transactions:** Names, contact details, and delivery addresses for processing and delivering merchandise orders.
- **Website Usage Data:** Information collected through cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience.

### **3\. Use of Personal Information**

We use the collected personal information to:

- Communicate with supporters, donors, and volunteers.
- Process and fulfill merchandise orders.
- Distribute newsletters and updates about our activities.
- Improve our website and services.

We do not share personal information with third parties, except as necessary for the above purposes or as required by law.

### **4\. Storage and Security of Personal Information**

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. This includes secure storage systems and restricted access to personal information.

### **5\. Cookies and Website Analytics**

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us understand website usage patterns. You can configure your browser to reject cookies, but this may affect website functionality.

### **6\. Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on our website with the updated effective date.
