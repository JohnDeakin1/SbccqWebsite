We have all been through a lot in the last year with Covid-19. Now is the time to re-ignite those passions, and ours is wildlife protection and conservation!

My name is Katy, and I am going to be writing these blog posts with Steph every week to share with you!

Here at SBCCQ we have had an exciting few developments since the end of last year. We have become incorporated officially as a not-for-profit which has been a long awaited happening! Now we can focus on some new projects. One of which is Stephanie's passion for a mobile phone game called "Stephanie's Animal Rescue". So we wanted to share how this project came about in this week's post.

Steph loves to play games to pass the time and found the reading components in the games challenging at times. She also wanted to play a game that could teach more children about conservation but in a fun and magical way! And so the idea of "Stephanie's Rescue Island" was born.

So every week since September 2020, Steph has met with myself to learn about animals from all over the world. There is so much to learn and so much has been discovered with Steph's amazing imagination and thirst for wildlife knowledge. Each week following this post, we will share our favourite animals for the week and a few fun facts and the current news and conservation status of the animal.

We can't wait to share this with you, catch you next time!
